Google Ads Audit On one page

The account works. What is broken is how it is built.

Google Ads is bringing in registrations at a cost that holds up, and improving month over month. The problem is how it is built: four campaigns buy the same searches from each other, the brand campaign spends half its money outside the brand, and the account does not tell new customers from returning ones, which is what matters when the goal is FTD. Each section states a problem and the action that fixes it.

We went through the account looking for searches with no commercial value (support, play for free, «is it trustworthy», PDFs, jobs). Five terms to read the rest

With these five concepts the whole document makes sense. Any other term is explained where it appears.

Keyword and search term

The keyword is what we tell Google we want to buy. The search term is what the person actually typed. They are not the same thing, and the distance between the two is where the money is lost.

Match type

How close what the person types has to be to what we bought. Exact: almost identical. Phrase: with words around it. Broad: Google decides by topic and may pull in distant things. 85% of the account is on broad.

Impression share

Out of the times someone searched where we could have appeared, how often we did. Google also says why we missed: lost to budget or lost to rank, and each needs a different fix.

Quality Score

A 1-to-10 grade Google gives each keyword based on how relevant the ad and the landing page are to that search. A low grade means paying more for the same click. It is a surcharge, not a label.

Cost per registration

How much has to be spent for one person to finish signing up. It is the number this entire audit is read against. The account is at 71,495 pesos today, and its best campaign at 46,732.

How it should look

Three campaigns with a boundary, each with intent-based ad groups and its own ad. The fourth is paused. Budgets are a starting point, not a destination: they move in steps while cost per registration is checked.

Brand

1.3M a day. It has 2.5M today
  1. Buys

    «yajuego» and its variants, on exact and phrase match. A single ad group: the brand territory is small and needs no more.

  2. Excludes

    Every competitor brand, every celebrity-fronted casino, and the generic casino and betting terms.

  3. Message

    The live offer, not institutional copy. This is the highest-return change in the whole audit.

Promotions

2.9M a day. Unchanged
  1. Buys

    No-deposit bonus, Nequi payments, games that pay out, live casino and roulette. Five ad groups, one per theme. And it picks up the competitor traffic it already converts well, at 51,496.

  2. Excludes

    The own brand, which moves to Brand. Competitors stay: here they convert at 51,496, not at 85,095 as in Brand.

  3. Message

    One ad per group, written around the specific offer that person searched for. Today all five share a single ad.

Generic

1.5M a day. Unchanged
  1. Buys

    Sports betting, bookmakers and football. Three ad groups. This is the campaign currently called Betting Terms.

  2. Excludes

    The own brand and the promotional terms, which belong to Promotions. Competitors stay: here they convert at 63,604.

  3. Message

    Category plus differentiator: why YaJuego and not anyone else. Distinct from the promotional message.

Competitors

Paused. It has 720K a day today
  1. Why

    It pays up to five times the market price because of its Quality Score of 1, and delivers registrations at 169,991 pesos.

  2. What happens to that demand

    Promotions and Generic already capture it incidentally, and convert it at 51,496 and 63,604 pesos.

  3. When it would come back

    Only with its own ads and landing page lifting the Quality Score. Not before that.

The boundary between campaigns does not exist today, and that is what makes nothing comparable.

Today there is no boundary: all four campaigns enter the same auction for the same search and Google picks one. That is why 91.3% of spend is contested between the account’s own campaigns and no cost per registration is comparable with another.

The problem: the account does not tell new customers from returning ones

The idea: we need to bid for people who have not made their first deposit (FTD), and the account does not do that today. Three campaigns bid on purchase (every bet from anyone) and eleven on registration, which does not set returning users aside. Google has a mode to bid only for new customers and no campaign uses it.

A common argument, and what is true in it

«If we drop purchase, we stop targeting people who are already inside YaJuego». True: purchase optimizes toward people who already bet. It works for retention, not for acquisition.

What it does not solve

Leaving only registration does not remove returning users either: they keep seeing and clicking the ads, they just do not «convert». By default, Google «bids equally for new and existing customers».

And the real goal is FTD

Registration is a proxy. Until first deposit reaches Google Ads, it is the best indicator; the target is to bid for the FTD of new customers.

What the account data says

How to read this chart. Each campaign has two bars: bets per 100 clicks (dark) and registrations per 100 clicks (yellow). Many bets and few registrations point to clicks from people who already have an account. Competitor, which targets new people, is the control.

Returning users do not arrive through «log in»

Existing-customer searches that name the brand (log in, withdraw, verify) add up to only 67,033 pesos a month, 0.04%. Most likely they arrive searching plain «yajuego». That is why excluding terms is not enough: Google has to be told who they are.

It asks for a customer list

It accepts Customer Match (direct upload, CRM or API), Analytics audiences and website or app lists. A minimum of 100 active members; below 1,000 it may perform worse.

It reduces, it does not eliminate

Google also detects customers on its own (up to 540 days), but admits it is «less comprehensive because users can remove cookies and opt out».

The problem: nothing limits what the account buys

The idea: there are two controls and the account uses neither. Match type decides which searches can trigger a keyword; exclusions decide which cannot. And the thematic list that looks obvious (free, support, «is it trustworthy») is not the right one: it converts better than average.

The two controls

How to read this diagram. A search goes through two doors before becoming a paid click. Boxes with a yellow border are the controls, and below each one, where the account stands today.

Match type

On broad, «casino» is triggered by thousands of different searches. 612 of the 617 keywords are on broad. On brand, the Google forecast gives 601 clicks on exact and 2,548 on broad: the extra clicks are not more people searching for YaJuego.

Exclusions

They tell Google that even if a search looks similar, the ad must not show. The account has zero. Without them, not even the strictest match type stops Brand from buying competitors.

Be sharper

Brand on exact and phrase; broad only in tests with a capped budget; and exclusion lists cross-applied between campaigns. The table assigns each flow its control.

«Is it trustworthy» is not a complaint

«nequi casino es legal», «yajuego es confiable»: someone verifying before signing up. 2.6 million a month at 63,058 per registration, better than average.

«Free» almost never means free

The real searches are «juegos gratis para ganar dinero real»: free to enter, not free of deposits. It is the best-converting theme (49,866). Excluding «gratis» wholesale would be the worst change.

What is genuinely waste: 140,408 pesos

31 terms with not one registration: «trucos para jugar en casinos», «hack mines 1win», «donde ver cali vs santa fe femenino», «términos y condiciones bono yajuego». It is 0.08% of spend: housekeeping, not a lever.

A fifth of the spend cannot be audited this way

The search campaigns spend 218.6 million a month and the search terms report shows 174.1. The other 44.5 million (20.4%) falls below the Google’s reporting threshold, and no exclusion touches it. That is why the structure gets fixed, not the terms.

The action: strict match types on brand and cross-exclusions, not a thematic list.

What gets done

  1. Move brand to exact and phrase; broad only in tests with a capped budget.

  2. Create four shared exclusion lists and cross-apply them: own brand out of Promotions and Generic; generics out of Brand; promotional terms out of Generic.

  3. Take competitors out of Brand, where they convert at 85,095; they stay in Promotions and Generic (51,496 and 63,604).

  4. Apply the short list of 31 valueless terms, as housekeeping.

How to verify

Spend on contested searches falls from 91.3% to under 30% in next month’s report.
