Google Ads Audit On one page

The account works. What is broken is how it is built.

Google Ads is bringing in registrations at a cost that holds up, and improving month over month. The problem is that four campaigns buy the same searches from each other, the brand campaign spends half its money outside the brand, and five of the sixteen campaigns are measured with a counter that does not count customers. Each section states a problem and the action that fixes it.

And a warning about something that looks like a problem and is not.

We went through the account looking for searches with no commercial value: people asking for support, looking to play for free, asking whether the casino is trustworthy, requesting PDFs or looking for jobs. Intuition says there is money wasted there. The data says otherwise: those searches convert better than the account average, and excluding them would remove results. What genuinely is waste comes to 140,408 pesos a month. The detail is in the exclusions section.

Five terms you need for the rest

This document avoids jargon where it can, but five concepts have no synonym. With these five, everything else follows.

Keyword and search term

The keyword is what we tell Google we want to buy. The search term is what the person actually typed. They are not the same thing, and the distance between the two is where the money is lost.

Match type

How close what the person types has to be to what we bought. Exact only triggers on almost the same wording. Phrase allows words around it. Broad lets Google decide by topic similarity, and can pull in things very far away. This account has 85% of its spend on broad.

Impression share

Out of every time someone searched where we could have appeared, how often we did. If it is 30%, we missed seven out of ten. Google also says why: lost to budget or lost to rank. That difference decides whether the fix is moving money or improving the ad.

Quality Score

A 1-to-10 grade Google gives each keyword based on how relevant the ad and the landing page are to that search. A low grade means paying more for the same click. It is a surcharge, not a label.

Cost per registration

How much has to be spent for one person to finish signing up. It is the number this entire audit is read against. The account is at 71,495 pesos today, and its best campaign at 46,732.

Two classification calls worth knowing about.

Betjuego counts as own brand, not as a competitor: it is the name of the GA4 property and is listed as an account alias. Celebrity-fronted casinos count as competitors: «cossio casino», «luis diaz casino» and similar are rival operators with a famous face, not a generic category. If either rule does not match the commercial criterion, the percentages can be recalculated in minutes, because the classification runs over the complete report rather than a sample.

How it should look

Three campaigns with a boundary, each with intent-based ad groups and its own ad. The fourth is paused. Budgets are a starting point, not a destination: they move in steps while cost per registration is checked.

1.3M a day. It has 2.5M today

  2. Excludes

The boundary between campaigns does not exist today, and that is what makes nothing comparable.

With no exclusions, all four campaigns enter the same auction for the same search and Google picks one. The result is that 91.3% of search spend falls on terms contested by two or more of the account's own campaigns, each campaign learns from half the information, and one campaign's cost per registration cannot be compared with another's. Setting the boundary is not tidiness for its own sake: it is the precondition for every other decision to have a basis.

The problem: the main counter does not count customers

Eleven campaigns chase the registration, which is correct. Five do not: three chase an event called purchase, one chases YouTube channel subscriptions and one chases app installs. The problem is with purchase: it is set to count every bet, so one person who bets eight times counts as eight. And most of those bets come from people who were already customers.

More conversions than clicks

The Demand Gen Manifesto / Purchase campaign records 9,917 conversions from 2,018 clicks. Nearly five «purchases» per click. That is not possible if each conversion were a new person.

The signal broke in May

The count fell from 281,016 to 45,624 between May and September without spend going down. A business result does not divide by six in four months: that is a measurement installation problem.

And it drags the whole year's reading

Added up, the account looks like it is collapsing: from 130,179 conversions in January to 23,644 in September. That is the broken counter. Looking only at registrations, the opposite happened: 1,698 to 2,859.

How to read this chart. Each bar is a month, and its length is how many times the purchase event fired. May is the highest month, so its bar takes the full width and the others are drawn in proportion to it. In red, the months where the count had already collapsed.

The action: have all sixteen campaigns chase the registration.

Change the conversion goal on the three campaigns currently chasing purchase, and decide whether YouTube and App join the same indicator or get reported separately against their own objective. In parallel, downgrade purchase from «counts every bet» to «counts one per person» and open a review with the measurement team about the May drop. Until that happens, no decision should rest on the aggregate conversions column. How to verify: those three campaigns should go back to reporting a six-figure cost per registration rather than a three-figure one.

The action: give it the Promotions ad and close its exit.

Two moves in the same campaign. First, duplicate inside Brand the ads Promotions already has validated, leaving the current ones running so the two can be compared at fourteen days. It costs nothing and is the highest-return change on the list. Second, move the brand variants from broad to phrase match and add exclusions for competitors and generics, which frees 32.4 million a month inside the campaign itself. Today Brand runs out of budget in 27.8% of the auctions for its own name while paying for other people's. How to verify: the registration rate on «yajuego» should rise above 4.5%, and non-brand spend should fall from 49% to under 10%.

The problem: there is not a single exclusion, but the obvious list is the wrong one

The account has no negative keywords at all. No shared lists, none at campaign level, none at ad group level. With 85% of spend on broad match, nothing stops any campaign from taking any search. The natural reaction is to build a list of themes with no commercial value. We checked it term by term, and the data says that list would remove results.

How to read this chart. Each bar is a theme you would expect to exclude, and its length is what a registration costs within that theme. The vertical line is the account average, 71,495 pesos: anything shorter than that line is converting better than average, and excluding it would be a mistake. In red, the only theme that produces no registrations at all.

«Is it trustworthy» is not a complaint

«nequi casino es legal», «cossio casino es real», «yajuego es confiable». This is not someone with a problem: it is someone about to sign up checking they will not be scammed. That is 2.6 million a month converting at 63,058 pesos, better than average. Excluding it would cut the last step before registration.

«Free» almost never means free

The actual searches are «juegos gratis para ganar dinero real» and «juegos gratis que pagan dinero real» — free games that pay out real money. The word refers to not paying to enter, not to avoiding a deposit. It is the best-converting of the five themes at 49,866 pesos. Excluding the word «gratis» wholesale would be the worst possible change.

What genuinely is waste comes to 140,408 pesos

Thirty-one terms with not one registration: «trucos para jugar en casinos», «hack mines 1win», «donde ver cali vs santa fe femenino», «términos y condiciones bono yajuego», «software para predecir resultados», and eleven searches from people trying to recover an account at a rival operator. Worth applying, but it is 0.08% of spend: housekeeping, not a lever.

And a fifth of the spend cannot be audited this way at all.

The search campaigns spend 218.6 million a month, but the search terms report only shows 174.1. The other 44.5 million, 20.4%, falls below Google's reporting threshold: these are searches so infrequent that Google does not list them individually. No exclusion list can touch them. It is the decisive argument for fixing the structure rather than chasing terms.

The action: cross-exclusions between campaigns, not a thematic list.

The real lever is not cutting themes but setting boundaries. Create four shared exclusion lists, one per territory, and cross-apply them: brand only enters Brand, promotional only enters Promotions, generics only enter Generic, competitors enter none. That sorts out the 91.3% of spend currently contested by several campaigns. Separately, and as housekeeping, apply the short list of 31 valueless terms. How to verify: spend on contested searches should fall from 91.3% to under 30% in next month's report.

The problem: the competitor campaign pays five times the market price

Competitor spends 21.7 million a month and brings in 142 registrations, at 152,963 pesos each. The cause is in the Quality Score: its main keywords score 1 out of 10, its ads are rated poor, and it only appears in 10% of auctions. A low score means a surcharge on every click, and here the surcharge is visible.

How to read this chart. The grey bar on each row is the market price range: from what the cheapest advertiser pays to what the most expensive pays to appear at the top of that search. The black dot is what YaJuego pays. When the dot sits to the right of the bar, we are paying more than anyone else in that market.

How to read this chart. All four campaigns buy competitor brand searches. Three do it without meaning to, through broad match. The bar measures what a registration costs in each one, and the campaign built for this is the longest.

The action: pause it, and spend a month checking whether it was needed.

Pause rather than repair, because fixing the Quality Score requires new ads and a new landing page, and the demand is already being picked up by another campaign at a third of the cost. It frees 21.7 million a month. The campaign also loses 76.2% of its impressions to budget, so adding money would only scale the problem. How to verify: at four weeks, whether spend and registrations on competitor searches hold up in the other three campaigns. If they hold, the campaign was contributing nothing of its own. If they fall, rebuild it with its own ads and landing page before switching it back on.

The action: grow through coverage, not budget.

Promotions does not scale by adding money, because it does not spend it. The problem: out of every hundred people who start registering, nine finish

The advertising brings 43,164 people a month to start the registration form. 3,762 finish it. The loss is not spread across the process: 86% goes in the first screen, before reaching the second step. This is not fixed with advertising, but it caps everything advertising can achieve.

How to read this chart. Each bar is a step of the registration, and its length is how many people reach it. The first bar is everyone who started. The drop between the first and the second is the leak.

The action: review the first screen with the product team, before optimizing bids further.

Today 14.0% of those who start reach step 2. Lifting that to 17% would add roughly 800 registrations a month, about 21% more than the account brings in today, with no additional spend. That there is room is something the account itself shows: in Promotions, 14.4% of those who start finish the registration, and in Brand only 7.3%, even though Brand brings the warmest audience. It is the same form; what changes is the expectation the person arrives with, and that points back at the ad.

Action plan

The same actions from each section, gathered and in execution order. The order is by dependency, not importance: without measurement fixed, budget decisions cannot be evaluated. Each line gives why it is done, how it is approached and exactly what gets touched.

  1. Have every campaign chase the registration

    Week 1 · Measurement

    Why. While five campaigns are measured with a different counter, their results cannot be compared with the rest or with the business objective.

    How. Unify the conversion goal across all sixteen campaigns on completed registration, and downgrade purchase to an observation metric.

    What gets touched. Conversion goal on Demand Gen Manifesto / Purchase, Demand Gen Bono Activa tu Suerte and PMax Beneficios YaJuego (new videos), which together are 19.7 million a month. Counting type on purchase, from «every bet» to «one per person». Review with the measurement team about the May drop.

  2. Give Brand the Promotions ad

    Week 1 · Brand

    Why. The warmest audience in the account receives the weakest message. The same term converts at 8.3% with an offer and 4.5% without one.

    How. Duplicate the already-validated ads rather than writing new ones, and leave the current ones running so the two can be compared.

    What gets touched. Responsive search ads inside the Brand ad group. No additional spend. At fourteen days, compare the registration rate on «yajuego».

  3. Close Brand's exit toward competitors and generics

    Week 2 · Brand

    Why. Brand runs out of budget in 27.8% of the auctions for its own name while sending 32.4 million a month to searches that do not name it.

    How. Tighten the match types and set the boundary, so the budget it already has stays inside brand territory.

    What gets touched. Move «Ya juego», «ya juego apuestas», «yajuego app», «ya juego casino» and «yajuego iniciar sesión» from broad to phrase. Exclusion list covering competitor brands and celebrity-fronted casinos.

  4. Split the territory between campaigns

    Week 2 · Structure

    Why. 91.3% of search spend falls on terms contested by two or more of the account's own campaigns. None of them learns from the complete picture.

    How. Four shared exclusion lists, one per territory, cross-applied. This is what does not exist today: the account has no exclusions at all.

    What gets touched. Brand only in Brand, promotional only in Promotions, generics only in Generic. Separately, the short list of 31 valueless terms, worth 140,408 pesos a month.

  5. Pause the competitor campaign

    Week 2 · Budget

    Why. It pays up to five times the market price because of its Quality Score of 1, and delivers registrations at 169,991 pesos when Promotions gets the same ones at 51,496.

    How. Pause rather than repair, and use the following four weeks as the test of whether it contributed anything of its own.

    What gets touched. Search Competitor goes to paused and frees 21.7 million a month. Watch whether competitor spend and registrations hold up in the other three campaigns.

  6. Split the single ad groups by intent

    Week 3 · Structure

    Why. Three campaigns worth 196.9 million a month have one ad group each, so someone searching «no-deposit bonus» and someone searching «yajuego log in» get the same message.

    How. Use the search clusters Keyword Planner has already validated as real demand, not as a hypothesis.

    What gets touched. Promotions splits into no-deposit bonus, Nequi payments, games that pay out, live casino and roulette. Generic splits into sports betting, bookmakers and football. Each group gets its own ad.

  7. Move the freed budget to what is starved

    Week 3 onward · Budget

    Why. The cheapest campaigns in the account run out of money, and the one with the most money does not spend it. The allocation is exactly backwards.

    How. In 30% weekly steps, and only where impressions are lost to budget rather than to rank.

    What gets touched. PMax Bono Activa tu Suerte from 350,000 to 455,000 a day, and PMax Juégala Toda from 50,000 to 65,000. Promotions gets no increase: the forecast shows 63% more budget buys it nine clicks. Verify cost per registration stays below 55,000 at each step.

  8. Put a ceiling on brand bidding

    Week 4 · Brand

    Why. With no target cost, the bidding strategy has no reason to stop, and on «yajuego casino» it already pays above the market ceiling.

    How. Set a target cost per registration and approach it in steps, without jumps, so the bidding does not stop competing.

    What gets touched. Start at 70,000 pesos, 25% below today's 87,497, and step down weekly toward the 41,661 Promotions already achieves. If daily delivery falls more than 15%, roll back the step.

  9. Widen the Promotions territory

    Week 4 · Coverage

    Why. It is the best-converting campaign and it cannot spend what it has. Its limit is territory, not money.

    How. Move into the territories Keyword Planner flags as real demand with low competition, which the account barely touches today.

    What gets touched. Online roulette, the mines game and Nequi payments, on phrase match inside their new ad groups. Verify the campaign moves to using more than 95% of its budget.

  10. Decide what happens to what does not deliver

    Week 4 · Budget

    Why. A line with no result against the agreed indicator is not a long-term bet until someone has decided it is one. If it serves another objective, that objective needs naming.

    How. Set a deadline and a criterion rather than letting them run on inertia. The decision is the client's, but it needs to be made.

    What gets touched. Demand Gen, YouTube and App together are 25.2 million a month for 71 registrations: either give them a registration target with a four-week deadline, or close them. Ruta del Campeón, live with 130,000 a day and zero impressions in thirty days, closes now.

What is not solved inside Google Ads

Two fronts that cap the result and depend on other teams.

  1. The first screen of the registration

    86% of those who start never reach the second screen. Three points of improvement there are worth roughly 800 registrations a month, more than any budget move on this list, and they cost nothing in media. Worth reviewing with the product team before tuning bids further.

  2. What a new customer is worth

    Until first deposit exists as an event in Google Ads, registration is the best available indicator, but it remains a proxy. When the commercial data arrives this audit will need rereading: most likely it changes the order between Promotions and Generic, and confirms the read on Competitor and Demand Gen.
